Dàn bài có 4 đoạn:  
Đoạn 1 - Mở bài: 2 câu, trong đó câu 1 là câu giới thiệu bối cảnh kết hợp với chủ đề có biến đổi 
từ theo đề bài, câu 2 là câu trả lời định hướng bài viết có nêu ý kiến.  
Đoạn 2 - Thân bài 1: 5 câu, thân bài 1 cần 2 ý chính để giải thích khía cạnh thuận lợi 
Đoạn 3 - Thân bài 2: 5 câu, thân bài 2 cần 2 ý chính để giải thích khía cạnh bất lợi  
Đoạn 4 – Kết bài: 1-2 câu để khẳng định, tổng kết lại các quan điểm đã nêu ở trên

nắm kĩ các cấu trúc thường dùng để viết bài như:

Tập trả lời theo một dàn bài mà bạn quen thuộc, sẽ dễ hơn nếu phân tích từng phương án và 
làm rõ ưu nhược điểm của từng phương án. Phương án được lựa chọn sẽ có nhiều lý do tích 
cực hơn. 
Bài nói mẫu B2:  
Well, if my friend wanted her English to be better, I would suggest her communicating with 
foreigners. There are some clear benefits following. 
First of all, when she talks to people from English speaking countries, it can help her improve 
her speaking skill. She also has a chance to hear many stories from different countries in the 
world, which can broaden her horizon. An other reason is that there are more and more 
foreigners visiting our city, so it’s very easy to find someone to practice English with, for 
example, she can go to some English cafés. 
Speaking of watching films to learn English, actually it’s a good way to expand her 
vocabulary, her listening skill can be improved a lot if she is a fan of it. However, I think 
it’s kind of difficult for her to understand because she’s just a beginner, she may be 
distracted from learning as well.  
Finally, I have to admit that reading books can help her improve her reading skill, but it 
needs self-discipline and concentration which my friend doesn’t have. So, this way may not 
for her. 
To sum up, I believe the best way to learn English for my friend is to practice with 
foreigners. 
self-discipline: tính kỉ luật 
concentration: sự tập trung

In the first place, online learning is very convenient. You know, students can study whenever 
and wherever they want. Only with a mobile phone or a computer connected to the Internet, they 
are able to get access to a rich source of information on almost any subjects that they’re 
interested in. 
Secondly, it is very affordable to study online. Well, students can do research using online 
resources at very low cost. Many websites even provide free access to their huge source of 
materials, this helps learners save a big amount of money when studying. 
An other benefit is that students can study at their own place, they can save time and money to 
travel to formal classes.  
Last but not least, online courses are more interesting thanks to the application of technology. It 
is true that technology is a powerful tool to engage students. It can make students more involved 
in the lesson due to the combination of sounds, images, games and so on. 
In conclusion, studying on the Internet brings considerable benefits to learners.
